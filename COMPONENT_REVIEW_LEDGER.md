## Status rules

- **Contracted · unverified**: source, PRD, documentation, framework, and deterministic evidence paths exist, but the complete proof bundle has not been reviewed.
- **In review**: a dependency wave is actively being audited. Partial evidence is recorded without implying completion.
- **Verified**: product necessity, applicable states, keyboard and touch, accessibility, reduced motion, desktop/intermediate/phone/200% zoom, light/dark, LTR/RTL, Chinese/English, HTML/React/Vue, screenshots, console, and page-error evidence all pass.
- Any regression returns a component to **In review**. An implementation or test pass is never a terminal state.

## Required proof bundle

1. Product decision and ownership boundary remain defensible.
2. Every applicable state has realistic minimum, typical, maximum, loading, empty, error, success, disabled, selected, and open content.
3. Mouse, keyboard, coarse pointer, focus entry/return, screen-reader naming, contrast, reduced motion, and recovery paths pass.
4. Desktop, intermediate, phone, 200% zoom, light, dark, RTL, Chinese, and English layouts remain usable.
5. HTML, React, Vue, hook/composable, source, manifest, bilingual docs, and tests describe the same semantic contract.
6. A3S Test screenshots, accessibility output, console output, and page-error output are manually reviewed and linked.
