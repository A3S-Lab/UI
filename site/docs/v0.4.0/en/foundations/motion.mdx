# Motion

Motion explains origin, continuity, or completion. It does not delay work or make static cards float. Elements begin in a usable, visible state so lower-performance devices never reveal an empty interface first.

## Durations

| Token                  | Duration | Use                                         |
| ---------------------- | -------: | ------------------------------------------- |
| `--a3s-motion-fast`    |  `120ms` | Press, focus, and short color feedback      |
| `--a3s-motion-normal`  |  `160ms` | Hover, selection, and small layout feedback |
| `--a3s-motion-overlay` |  `220ms` | Menus, popovers, dialogs, and drawers       |

Continuous pointer interactions such as Split Pane resizing and Slider dragging follow input directly without a catch-up transition. Pressed controls may move by at most 1px; cards do not lift on hover.

## Easing

Entry and reversible feedback use the exponential ease-out token `--a3s-ease-out: cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`. Exit may reuse the curve at a shorter duration, but never exceeds 220ms. Avoid long simultaneous transitions across layout, blur, and shadow.

## Reduced motion

Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, remove travel, scale, blur animation, and looping decoration while preserving immediate state changes. Loading, streaming, and progress must remain understandable without animation.
